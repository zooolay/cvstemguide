About

The resource that should have already existed.

CV STEM Guide collects STEM programs, scholarships, and competitions from across 18 Central Valley counties into one place — so students don't have to dig through dozens of websites to find opportunities actually available to them.

The gap

Why the Central Valley needs this

The Central Valley is one of the most underserved regions in California for STEM access. More than 50% of students here identify as economically disadvantaged, and most schools report a shortage of advanced STEM programs. The opportunities exist — they're just hard to find. CV STEM Guide exists to close that information gap, for free, forever.

The creator

Built by a high school student from Patterson, California

Growing up in the Central Valley and seeing firsthand how few STEM resources were visible or accessible to local students, the goal was simple: build the resource that should have already existed. If this site helped you find a program or scholarship, share it with a classmate, a counselor, or a local library. The more students who know it exists, the more useful it becomes.
