Contribute

Add a resource. Help a student.

Every addition helps another Central Valley student find an opportunity they would have otherwise missed. Submissions are reviewed and added on a rolling basis.

Gather the details

Program name, the organization, an official link, eligible grades, cost, counties served, and a short 2–3 sentence description.

Submit it

Open an issue on GitHub or email the details. Whichever is easier for you.

It goes live

Submissions are reviewed and added on a rolling basis, usually within a couple of weeks.
