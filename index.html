Why this exists

One place for opportunities that are actually within reach.

The Central Valley is one of the most underserved regions in California for STEM access. More than half of local students are economically disadvantaged, and most schools report a shortage of advanced STEM programs. CV STEM Guide closes that information gap.

Source for the economic-disadvantage figure: California free / reduced-price school-meal eligibility data via Kidsdata.org (Lucile Packard Foundation) and the California Department of Education (Student Poverty FRPM data). Central Valley counties report among the state's highest rates (many districts 60–85%); exact figures vary by district and year.
